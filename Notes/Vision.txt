CROOKED GALAXY — VISION

Crooked Galaxy is a stylized comedic sci-fi idle RPG focused on the fantasy of becoming one of the galaxy’s greatest bounty hunters.

CORE PILLARS

1. BOUNTY HUNTER FANTASY

The player is an interstellar bounty hunter.

The main progression loop is:

Inspect Bounties
→ Choose Contract
→ Hunt
→ Encounter Target
→ Automatic Combat
→ Receive Reward
→ Collect Loot
→ Equip Better Gear
→ Become Stronger
→ Raise Hunter Level
→ Discover New Worlds and Harder Bounties

The player should constantly feel that they are building a bounty hunting career.

2. STYLIZED / COMEDIC SCI-FI

The universe should be colorful, strange, dangerous and funny.

The tone is:

stylized

* comedic
* adventurous
* occasionally epic

The game can contain:

* bizarre aliens;
* ridiculous criminals;
* strange technology;
* unusual weapons;
* corrupt corporations;
* shady stations;
* weird planets;
* space-western influences;
* humorous item and bounty descriptions.

The game should not become hard military sci-fi or generic realistic science fiction.

3. PLANETS AS AN EXPANDING MISSION NETWORK

Planets are destinations, lore, visual identity and content pools rather than a mandatory linear career map. The bounty board always offers three different targets; as hunter level rises, new worlds and criminal families enter that rotating network. A destination's distance creates the travel portion of a mission and gives transport progression lasting value. Canonical base routes are 5, 8, 12, 16, and 20 minutes; transparent starter acceleration scales travel to 40%, 60%, and 80% through levels 1–12 before the full route applies from level 13. Transports permanently reduce travel only by 10%, 20%, 30%, or 50%; pursuit remains additive. Hunt incidents are optional opportunities during the same authoritative wall-clock route: ignoring one has no consequence and never pauses arrival, while choosing an explicit detour may add its advertised duration.

Enemy combat statistics are fixed when an offer is created from its hunter-level band and risk role. They must never scale from currently equipped power: replacing equipment must make the same accepted offer genuinely easier. Planets can still introduce:

* new species;
* new bounty targets;
* new enemies;
* new equipment;
* new visual themes;
* new dungeon/event types;
* new stories;
* new mechanics.

Planet archives, mastery and recorded captures preserve the feeling of exploring and completing content without forcing every hunter through the same sequential target chain. Independent ladders, special contracts and future events may use a specific world as context without turning the galaxy back into one vertical campaign.

The galaxy should feel almost unlimited.

A planet can be:

* desert;
* frozen;
* oceanic;
* mechanical;
* fungal;
* cyberpunk;
* living;
* ruined;
* prison-based;
* controlled by machines;
* inside an alien megastructure.

Almost nothing is too strange if it fits the Crooked Galaxy tone.

GAME TYPE

Crooked Galaxy is primarily:

* idle / AFK RPG;
* character progression RPG;
* loot-driven RPG;
* asynchronous online RPG.

Combat is mainly automatic.

The player’s decisions come from:

* bounty selection;
* equipment;
* builds;
* upgrades;
* progression;
* risk/reward choices;
* future PvP and Syndicate systems.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Target style:

2D hand-drawn cartoon/comic sci-fi.

Characters should use:

* caricatural proportions;
* expressive faces;
* bold outlines;
* simple shapes;
* flat colors;
* limited cel shading;
* low-to-medium detail;
* strong mobile readability.

The game should prioritize personality over realism.

Character and equipment art should eventually use a modular paper-doll system where practical.

PLAYER PROGRESSION

Important long-term systems may include:

* Character Level;
* Bounty Rank / Reputation;
* equipment;
* archetypes;
* species;
* planets;
* inventory;
* ship progression;
* Arena;
* rankings;
* Syndicates;
* global events.

Not all of these belong in the first MVP.

PLAYER ENTRY AND IDENTITY

Opening the game must lead through a mandatory identity flow before the bounty board:

Login
→ Choose Class
→ Choose Species
→ Choose Hunter Name
→ Enter the Game

Login owns account/session identity. Class, species and hunter name belong to the character profile and must not be silently invented by a default save.

Crooked Galaxy is an international, multilingual game. User-facing copy must migrate to stable translation keys instead of treating Portuguese literals as the final source architecture. Portuguese and English are the first localization targets; a language may only be advertised as available after the complete playable surface is translated and regression-tested.

Accounts and characters belong to a server shard. The first world is International 1, a global multilingual server. Local test builds must preserve and display that server identity while stating honestly that no online connection or authoritative backend exists yet; local state must never pretend to be live server state.

Account provider, session, shard, locale and character ownership are separate identities. Device-authoritative test saves advance a local transaction revision but never claim authentication or synchronization. A future backend must compare acknowledged revisions and stop on divergent progress; currencies, inventory, claimed rewards and active phases must never be merged heuristically.

A new player cannot inspect or accept bounties until all four stages are complete. Each choice must be explicitly previewed and confirmed, and an interrupted creation flow must resume at the first incomplete stage rather than skipping ahead or losing earlier choices.

The three initial classes and eight initial species are the launch design baseline, while the unlimited setting may add new identities later. The eight initial species are visual and narrative identity only: species must never alter attributes, combat formulas, loot, economy or progression. The universal nine-slot inventory remains independent of both class and species.

Enemy families should create readable, class-neutral build questions rather than merely increasing numbers. Their strengths and weaknesses must be visible before combat, and universal equipment should let every current or future class respond in more than one coherent way.

FIRST PRODUCT GOAL

The first playable version should prove one thing:

Completing a bounty, receiving loot and becoming stronger should make the player want to take another bounty.

Do not build the entire galaxy before this loop is fun.

MONETIZATION GUARDRAILS

Crooked Galaxy is free-to-play without advertisements, season passes, loot boxes or mandatory subscriptions. Monetization may sell bounded time reduction, additional choices and honest convenience, but never levels, attribute points, combat probability, victory, superior exclusive equipment, mechanically superior species/classes or post-result combat rerolls.

Credits buy ordinary equipment and permanent progression. Scrap funds the workshop. Warp Chips are the premium currency and must also have controlled playable sources. Premium market refreshes sell another selection while the revealed item still costs Credits; they never guarantee an upgrade. Daily costs and limits must be visible before purchase. Real-money transactions require server-authoritative identity, wallet, time, receipts and idempotency; device-local builds are simulations only.

Daily retention must reward the core bounty loop without becoming a compulsory checklist. The initial daily shift uses only ordinary completed hunts, caps at five, excludes premium actions and Fenda, and pays only bounded normal resources. It must remain possible to start another hunt without visiting or claiming the daily screen.

PRODUCT SUMMARY

Crooked Galaxy is a colorful, humorous sci-fi RPG where the player builds an interstellar bounty hunter, tracks bizarre criminals across increasingly strange planets, collects alien loot and rises through the ranks in a galaxy full of crooked people, crooked systems and crooked worlds.
