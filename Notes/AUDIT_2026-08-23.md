# Crooked Galaxy — living audit (refreshed 2026-08-24)

## Reference boundaries

The local Shakes & Fidget material remains study-only under `References/` and is excluded from Godot imports. Crooked Galaxy does not reuse its proprietary code, formulas, names, text, protocols, interface, or assets.

The useful general observations remain limited to product principles: repeatable loot needs legible comparison, long progression benefits from stable collections and feature hubs, and large catalogs benefit from reusable visual/data boundaries. Crooked Galaxy implements these ideas independently through original rules, content, procedural presentation, stable IDs, and deterministic validation.

## Current product state

- The complete bounty loop is playable: board, approach, timed hunt, incident, automatic combat, reward, loot decision, workshop, and stronger contracts.
- Five four-target chapters provide twenty original wanted targets, ten planet-specific incidents, bosses, equipment families, progression, and persistent travel.
- Repeat motivation includes capture streaks, three mastery tiers per target, improved loot quality, one-time mastery scrap funding, career milestones, AFK patrol rewards, and a persistent wanted archive that recommends the closest unfinished mastery tier.
- Loot supports rarity, four combat-relevant modifications, planet of origin, and a two-piece planetary kit bonus. Item comparison evaluates the complete build rather than raw power alone.
- The arsenal provides filters, sorting, explicit tradeoffs, item protection, two loadouts, safe bulk recycling, power calibration, and capped integrity reinforcement with partial investment recovery.
- The arsenal's field test translates both workshop paths into projected odds against the next warrant before the player spends scrap, holding the briefing's currently recommended approach fixed for a fair comparison.
- Mobile presentation uses safe areas, large touch targets, vertical inventory scrolling, procedural portraits/backdrops, and runtime-synthesized audio.

## Engineering state

- `game_state.gd` owns the phase machine and persisted transactions; deterministic rules are isolated in focused modules.
- `ui_factory.gd` owns shared presentation primitives; `arsenal_view.gd`, `reward_view.gd`, and `career_view.gd` own the complete inventory/workshop, loot-decision, and career/archive renderers.
- Saves use explicit incremental migrations and preserve interrupted briefing, hunt, combat, reward, chapter, loadout, and workshop state.
- `tools/check_project.ps1` runs sixteen deterministic suites and a clean boot. Each run now uses isolated log files, while persistence tests use unique workspace-local saves so open Godot instances cannot corrupt test results. Dedicated balance guards protect the onboarding win, early risk window, safe approach, tactical pair, and planetary-kit payoff.
- Balance simulation executes the same damage helpers as runtime combat and verifies displayed odds against 5,000-fight samples.
- A separate 100-career simulation follows the first chapter's mastery path through real loot generation, automatic upgrade decisions, XP, and recommended approaches, exposing the loot-only upper bound without conflating it with individual fight calibration.
- A continuous 20-career campaign simulation carries real loot, XP, mastery scrap, workshop spending, recommended approaches, and sampled defeats through all five chapters.
- Automated captures cover the main loop, reward decisions, career/archive, arsenal, galaxy, incidents, and all five chapter finales.

## Findings addressed during this audit

1. Equipment modifications existed, but the two slots still behaved mostly as isolated stat containers. New opening-shot and incoming-damage traits now create distinct tactical identities.
2. Loot origin was flavor-only. Every newly generated item now records its planet, and matching weapon plus armor activates an explicit `+1 power / +6 health` planetary kit.
3. Raw item comparison could discard a weaker-looking piece that improved the complete build. Reward recycling, bulk recycling, upgrade labels, combat odds, and simulator decisions now evaluate the equipped build and its kit transition.
4. The 450×800 arsenal capture exposed horizontal clipping and a collapsed inventory area. Workshop and loadout cards were compacted, sorting received its own row, item comparisons moved into the flexible details column, and mobile tests now enforce horizontal bounds plus useful inventory height.
5. Concurrent Godot instances shared default logs and fixed test saves. The checker and persistence suite now isolate both resources per process.
6. Early planet progression was visible only beneath the contract list, and repeating a threshold capture could bypass the newly unlocked warrant. The next-target objective now sits above the board, reward screens preview exact capture progress, and threshold rewards route the first visit back to the expanded board with the new target named explicitly.
7. Automatic combat exposed only the latest exchange, so players could not tell what their build contributed. Combat now accumulates exact turns, damage dealt/received, criticals, mitigation, opening bonus, and active kit; victory shows a compact report, while defeat keeps a red diagnostic on the board with the target's remaining health. Round state is saved as it advances so this evidence also survives interruption.
8. Reward rendering had become a dense branch inside the main phase controller. It now lives in `reward_view.gd` with explicit state/host dependencies and isolated coverage for normal repetition, threshold unlock routing, board return, and safe instant recycling; `main.gd` is back near one thousand lines.
9. Workshop buttons exposed prices and raw stat gains but not their practical value. The arsenal now compares current, `+1 power`, and `+8 health` builds against the next warrant. Same-target projections share a deterministic combat roll stream, so genuine upgrades cannot appear worse because of Monte Carlo noise; the 5,000-fight simulator remains aligned with displayed odds.
10. The wanted archive documented mastery but did not convert it into a next action. Career rules now select the closest unfinished mastery tier, the screen explains the next rare/epic loot bonus, and a direct action opens that target's contract briefing. The same route remains highlighted on the bounty card after returning to the board. Reward screens count the pending capture, preview the next mastery threshold, and announce a level before it is claimed. The touched renderer moved from `main.gd` into `career_view.gd` with isolated coverage.
11. A scripted repeat path across 100 deterministic careers found a 99% median arrival chance after the first mastery tier when every real loot upgrade is equipped. A failure-aware pass initially measured defeats in 8% of Gloop careers, 7% at Barão, 4% at Madame, and 3% at the boss, while the workshop median remained at zero upgrades until Madame. Raising warrant stats collapsed separately calibrated build scenarios, so that trial was rejected. Instead, each newly earned mastery tier now grants a one-time 6/10/14 scrap reward: the simulator shows one workshop action before Barão, two before Madame, and three before the boss, while all combat guard rails remain unchanged. The reward is previewed in a mobile-readable two-line block, exact in board feedback, and can route directly into the funded workshop—even when the same capture reveals a new warrant—without removing the direct board route.
12. Entering the workshop from a combined mastery/warrant unlock exposed a target-selection error: the field test skipped the newly opened uncaptured warrant and projected the following locked tier. It now keeps the current highest available target until its first capture, then advances to the next warrant; the card labels the distinction as `MANDADO ATUAL` versus `PRÓXIMO MANDADO`.
13. Arsenal transactions relied on changing totals and button states for feedback, so the mastery funding message disappeared on arrival. A compact workshop record now carries the latest exact state notice through mastery routing, equipment changes, recycling, loadouts, and upgrades.
14. Field-test odds still required the player to compare four different workshop costs mentally. Every affordable power or integrity action is now simulated against the focused warrant; exactly one button receives a star for the best odds gain per scrap, with complete-build gain as a deterministic tie-breaker when displayed odds are saturated.
15. After preparing a newly opened warrant, the player still had to return to the board and find it again. When the field-test target is available and uncaptured, the panel now links directly to its briefing; future locked targets remain projections without an invalid action.
16. Alternate-strategy simulation exposed that nine Gloop captures could unlock the boss with a 95% median theoretical win chance, bypassing both intermediate warrants. Planet tiers now advance sequentially: three captures of the current prerequisite target unlock the next warrant. A repeated 30-career comparison unlocks the boss in 100% of mastery-route runs and 0% of sample-then-farm or Gloop-only runs. Total planet captures remain career statistics, while board and reward progress name the exact target required. The obsolete total-capture tier helpers were removed to prevent two contradictory progression models, and completed planets remain pinned to tier 3 for legacy-save compatibility.
17. The first continuous four-planet sample found no progression wall, but did expose long-campaign power inflation: median arrival power rises from 12 to 46 across Dustball, then reaches 77, 110, and 155 at successive chapter bosses, with nearly every recommended contract at 99%. This is now the blocking product evidence against adding a fifth chapter—not a technical blocker—and the campaign simulator records odds, low-odds incidence, power, and health at every target for controlled curve work.
18. The long-campaign saturation came from a hidden feedback loop: approach-modified enemy power also inflated dropped equipment, so the routinely recommended fast route financed its own future power advantage. Loot tier now uses the canonical target power; approaches alter the current encounter and advertised credits/XP only. A trial that additionally escalated risky multipliers by planet was rejected after telemetry drove both late alternatives as low as 1%. With canonical loot and stable multipliers, the 20-career sample reaches the final boss with `REDE SILENCIOSA` at 99%, `ESCOTILHA` around 60%, and `MANDADO CORPORATIVO` around 16%; the recommender genuinely alternates between safe and fast instead of seeing three saturated options. The campaign report now records the selected approach and all three arrival odds at every warrant so this distinction remains visible.
19. Once loot inflation was removed, the corporate warrant had no workshop identity despite carrying the highest combat risk. A successful corporate capture now pays two explicit scrap: three such wins equal the first +6 mastery grant, so the repeatable option complements rather than eclipses the milestone. Briefing, recommendation value, reward preview, state transaction, toast, and board record all account for it. The campaign simulator now compares the normal recommendation against an opportunistic corporate strategy and reports both corporate scrap and resulting workshop actions. In the deliberately success-assuming upper-bound cohort, the strategy earns 58 corporate scrap and completes 39 workshop actions versus 32 on the recommended route, yet arrives at the final boss with slightly lower median power (146 versus 148) because its XP tradeoff remains active; it improves build agency without dominating progression.
20. Workshop field tests still measured the canonical target while the actual briefing asked the player to choose a modified contract, making late-boss projections saturate and disagree with the next screen. Approach evaluation is now centralized in `contract_rules.gd` and shared by briefing, simulator, and arsenal. The workshop fixes the currently recommended approach while comparing all upgrade outcomes, names it explicitly, and still opens the canonical target briefing. A boss-ready Omega profile now projects `ENTRADA PELA ESCOTILHA` at 74%, +1 power at 75%, and +8 integrity at 81%; the dedicated 450×800 capture keeps the complete card and actions legible.
21. The continuous campaign previously assumed every displayed attempt won. It now samples defeat from the same deterministic combat odds, resets capture streaks, reevaluates approaches when that reset can change expected return, retries each capture up to a hard safety limit, and grants loot/workshop progress only after success. Across 20 careers per strategy, neither the recommended route nor the opportunistic corporate route stalls. The recommended cohort reaches Omega at 95% median arrival, then actually fights at 89% median (57% minimum) with 15% of careers seeing a boss defeat before recovery. The corporate cohort earns 62 median scrap and 39 workshop actions, still has 0% stalls, and reaches the boss at 93% arrival / 81% attempted median. Per-attempt approach and odds telemetry also exposes rare transient states below the 55% recommendation floor when every available route is weak, rather than hiding them behind arrival medians.
22. The failure-aware endpoint supported a fifth chapter without changing the validated progression machinery. Cassino Quasar adds four original warrants, two probability-themed incidents, a neon backdrop, a complete equipment family, four procedural portraits, a chapter milestone, and persistent travel through the existing catalogs. Initial boss health produced occasional sub-55% attempts; tuning reduced only fifth-chapter health. The full 20-career sample then completed both strategies with 0% stalls. Recommended play made no attempt below 55%, alternated quiet/fast routes throughout the chapter, and saw 30% of careers lose at least once to A Casa Eterna before recovery. Opportunistic corporate play earned 68 median scrap and 44 workshop actions without dominating final power, made no fifth-chapter attempt below 55%, and also remained stall-free. Dedicated 450×800 captures verify the board, incident, finale, and full twenty-character portrait sheet without clipping; static boss-ready guards now protect the intended safe/fast/corporate spread.
23. Growing to twenty targets made the career ledger a thirty-plus-card scroll with no direct archive navigation, while galaxy cards described every unlocked destination merely as an open route. The career header now provides 48-unit progress/archive shortcuts and keeps the mastery action visible above either destination. Galaxy cards distinguish completed chapters from open routes and name the current warrant plus its exact capture requirement. 24. The threshold reward announced a newly revealed warrant but forced the player to choose between going straight there and visiting the workshop without any risk context. It now evaluates the post-loot build with the same centralized approach rules as the briefing and displays the recommended route plus projected win chance before the loot decision. The label explicitly distinguishes an equipped upgrade from the unchanged build, preserving truthful guidance when the item is not actually better.

The diagnostic card now links directly to the field-test workshop, where the same active warrant and recommended approach drive upgrade projections. The 44-unit action remains inside mobile bounds and preserves the visible rematch card below it.

During defeat recovery this could replace the failed contract with a still-locked future tier, breaking the promised diagnosis→upgrade→revenge loop. Coverage reproduces the real post-defeat state with an empty transient bounty, plus the ambiguous one-capture tier, and verifies that the defeated target wins focus even after state restoration.
